Dear Parents,

Welcome to Treasureland Elementary School, where we are dedicated to nurturing young minds and fostering a love of learning. As the school's Director and Proprietor, I am delighted to share a few words with you.

Together with Mr. R.A. Ogunyemi, whose vision helped establish Treasureland, we are committed to building a strong educational foundation and an environment where every child feels valued, supported, and inspired to achieve their potential.

At Treasureland Elementary School, we are committed to providing a holistic education that goes beyond academic excellence. We strive to develop well-rounded individuals who are not only knowledgeable but also compassionate, creative, innovative, and resilient. Our dedicated team of educators works tirelessly to ensure that each pupil receives personalised attention and opportunities to explore their interests and talents.

We are proud of the progress of our school community, and our journey is far from over. We continue to grow and adapt to meet the changing needs of our pupils and the world around us.

Thank you for entrusting us with your child's education. We are honored to be a part of their journey and look forward to many more years of growth, learning, and success together.

Warm regards,

Mrs. E.E. Ogunyemi
Director and Proprietor, Treasureland Elementary School
