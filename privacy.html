Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us through this website.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who we are

Treasureland Elementary School is a primary school at Block 37, JDPC Road, Off Stadium Road, GRA, Ijebu Ode, Ogun State, Nigeria. For anything in this policy, contact us at hello@treasurelandschools.com or call +234 807 455 2156.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into a form on this website:

  • Tour and enquiry forms: your name, email address, phone number, preferred visit date and time, and anything you write in the message field.
  • Registration of interest: your child's first and last name, the class you are interested in, your name, phone number and email address.
  • Fee schedule requests: your name, email address and class of interest.

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels or analytics profiles on this website.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry, arrange a campus tour, send the fee schedule you asked for, and process a registration of interest. We do not use your details for unrelated marketing, and we do not sell or rent them to anyone.

Who can see it

Only the school staff who need to respond to you. Our forms are delivered by Formspree (formspree.io), a form-processing service that transmits submissions to the school's email inbox and stores them on our behalf. Our website is hosted on servers controlled by the school's hosting provider.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an application are kept for up to 12 months. Registration-of-interest records for an admissions cycle are kept until that cycle closes, then deleted unless you ask us to keep them for the following year.

Children's information

A child's name is only requested on the registration-of-interest form, and it should be submitted by a parent or guardian. We use it solely to identify the application. We never contact a child directly using details from this website.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email hello@treasurelandschools.com and we will respond within 30 days. You can also withdraw consent for us to contact you at any time.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies of its own. Embedded content such as the Google Map on our contact page is provided by Google and may set its own cookies once loaded.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. This policy was last reviewed on 12 November 2026.
