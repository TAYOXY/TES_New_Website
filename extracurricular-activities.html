Discover, create, and play

Extra-curricular Activities

A range of creative, practical, performing, and sporting activities gives pupils more ways to explore their interests and develop new skills.

Explore activities
Learning beyond the classroom

Find something to enjoy

From creative expression and practical skills to team sports and classic games, explore the activities available at Treasureland.

Music Club

Discover rhythm, melody, singing, and musical expression while building confidence and listening skills.

Art & Craft Club

Experiment with colours, shapes, and mixed media to create unique artworks and develop creative thinking.

Tie & Dye Club

Create vibrant fabric designs using folding, twisting, and dyeing techniques for unique wearable art.

Culinary Arts Club

Blend creativity, teamwork, and practical kitchen skills while exploring measuring, mixing, and healthy food preparation.

Makeup Artistry Club

Learn beauty skills, makeup techniques, and gele styling.

Dance Club

Move to music, learn dance steps, and express ideas through movement and performance.

Sports & Games Club

Build teamwork, coordination, and fitness through football, volleyball, athletics, and traditional games.

Seamstress & Sewing Club

Learn stitching, pattern-making, and garment construction from basics to creative projects.

Jet Club

Explore aviation, travel, and skiing through age-appropriate projects and club activities.

Hair Making Club

Braiding, natural haircare & Creative hairstyling.
