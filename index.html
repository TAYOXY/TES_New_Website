What makes a TES education different

Three things we do deliberately, every day

Not slogans — habits. These are the choices that shape how children learn here.

Thinking, not memorising

Children explain their reasoning, ask questions and work through problems instead of copying answers.

Character alongside academics

Respect, responsibility and resilience are taught explicitly, not left to chance.

Learning by doing

Hands-on projects and real materials — Montessori apparatus in Early Years, coding and robotics in Primary.
